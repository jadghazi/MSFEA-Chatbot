# Final Presentation Slide Template

This is the MSFEA PowerPoint template students fill in for the Approved
Experience final presentation. It provides the slide structure below (a title
slide, five content slides, and a closing slide). The maximum is 5 content
slides; see the presentation requirements in the report templates and the summer
training guidelines.

## Title Slide

- Title of presentation
- Speaker name and title
- Label: "Final Presentation — Advanced Experience", American University of Beirut

## Content Slides

- **Slide 1 — Internship Overview:** company; internship position; duration; main responsibilities.
- **Slide 2 — Major Project or Technical Contribution:** project description; role; objectives.
- **Slide 3 — Engineering/Design Knowledge Applied:** tools used; methods applied; problem-solving examples.
- **Slide 4 — Key Learning Outcomes:** technical learning; professional skills; challenges overcome.
- **Slide 5 — Reflection and Takeaways:** most valuable lesson; career insights; advice for future interns.

An alternative, more visual slide layout is also provided in the template to
replace any of the above.

## Closing Slide

- "Thank you"
