## Moodle access and registration

**Department:** All departments

**Question/topic:** My petition was approved, but I am not on the Moodle page.

**Answer:** Email the department coordinator with your full name, student ID, and the approved petition or approval email. Moodle access may need to be added manually when registration or the class list has not yet synchronized.

**Question/topic:** My name is not showing on AUBSIS. Can I still be added to Moodle?

**Answer:** Moodle access and AUBSIS registration are separate administrative checks. Contact the coordinator with the approval documents. The course team can check Moodle access, but AUBSIS registration must be resolved through the appropriate academic or Registrar process.

## Moodle submissions and corrections

**Department:** All departments

**Question/topic:** A Final Report or Final Presentation submission box is restricted even though the Progress Report is graded Pass.

**Answer:** First confirm that all required earlier activities are complete and that the Progress Report shows Pass. If the restriction remains, email the coordinator with a screenshot showing the Pass grade and the restriction.

**Question/topic:** Can I edit a form after submitting it on Moodle?

**Answer:** Students usually cannot edit a submitted form directly. Contact the coordinator, identify the form, and explain the correction. The submission may need to be reopened or deleted before resubmission.

**Question/topic:** I cannot upload a required file to Moodle.

**Answer:** Check the file format, file size, deadline, and whether a prerequisite activity is incomplete. If the problem continues, take a screenshot and email the coordinator before the deadline.

**Question/topic:** I entered the wrong dates or supervisor in a Moodle form.

**Answer:** Email the coordinator with the correct information and identify the form. The incorrect submission may need to be reopened or deleted before resubmission.

## Problems or changes during an internship

**Department:** All departments

**Question/topic:** My assigned work is not technical or no longer matches the approved internship.

**Answer:** Report the issue immediately to the internship coordinator and, when applicable, the CDC. Do not wait until the internship ends because the experience may no longer meet the approved objectives.

**Question/topic:** My internship supervisor changed after I started.

**Answer:** Inform the coordinator immediately and provide the new supervisor's name, title, and email. The Notice of Arrival and, if necessary, the Proposal may need to be reopened or resubmitted.

**Question/topic:** I changed companies after my internship was approved.

**Answer:** A new company or substantially different experience requires new approval. Contact the coordinator and CDC immediately, submit the required petition and company documents, and do not assume the previous approval carries over.

**Question/topic:** The company cancelled my internship.

**Answer:** Notify the coordinator and CDC immediately. An alternative approved arrangement and updated documentation are required. Keep the cancellation email or letter as supporting evidence.

## Internship dates and duration changes

**Department:** All departments

**Question/topic:** Can an internship be longer than eight weeks?

**Answer:** Yes. An approved internship may be longer than eight weeks. The Proposal, Notice of Arrival, and final employer letter should show the correct dates.

**Question/topic:** My internship end date is not fixed.

**Answer:** Enter the best date currently confirmed by the employer. When the date is finalized or changes, inform the coordinator and update or resubmit the relevant Moodle form if requested. The final employer letter must show the actual start and end dates.

**Question/topic:** My internship starts late or ends after the normal course deadline.

**Answer:** The approved duration must still be completed. Inform the coordinator before the deadline. Submit the Proposal when the internship is confirmed and the Notice of Arrival after starting. Final documents may be due within one week after completion, but the coordinator must confirm the current-term and graduation arrangements.

## Employer and research documentation exceptions

**Department:** All departments

**Question/topic:** The company refuses to issue an employer completion letter.

**Answer:** Inform the coordinator immediately and provide written evidence of the refusal. Ask whether an official supervisor email or another authorized document can be accepted. Do not substitute an informal document without approval.

**Question/topic:** Can a professor confirm a research internship?

**Answer:** When the research experience is approved, the faculty supervisor's letter or email may confirm its duration, the technical nature of the work, and the student's participation. Follow any additional Moodle reporting requirements.

**Question/topic:** My supervisor is unavailable to complete a required form or letter.

**Answer:** Ask another authorized company representative, such as HR or the supervisor's manager, whether they can issue the official document. Contact the coordinator before substituting a different signatory or document.

## Forms for additional internship components

**Department:** All departments

**Question/topic:** Which forms are used for a second internship?

**Answer:** Use the Moodle forms specifically labelled for a second internship when available, such as Proposal - Second Internship ONLY and Notice of Arrival - Second Internship ONLY. Submit the Proposal when the internship is confirmed and the Notice of Arrival after starting.

**Question/topic:** What form is used for a two-week research component?

**Answer:** Use the research two-week form provided on Moodle. Include the faculty supervisor, research dates, and technical nature of the work. Written confirmation from the faculty supervisor may also be required.

## Moodle quiz access and attempts

**Department:** All departments

**Question/topic:** I cannot access the internship Moodle quiz.

**Answer:** The quiz may remain restricted until all assigned lectures and recorded videos are completed or attempted. Complete the required resources and check again.

**Question/topic:** How many quiz attempts are allowed, or what happens if I do not reach the passing score?

**Answer:** Multiple attempts may be available so students can reach the required score. Follow the attempt settings on the current Moodle page. If the quiz is closed or no attempt remains, contact the coordinator.

**Question/topic:** I missed the quiz deadline.

**Answer:** Reopening is not automatic. Contact the coordinator promptly, explain the reason, and provide supporting information when applicable. A reopening requires an approved exception.

## Progress Report length and component

**Department:** All departments

**Question/topic:** How long should the Progress Report be?

**Answer:** Write approximately 1,000 words or 3-5 pages; these are equivalent length guidelines. Follow the current Moodle template and keep the report concise and focused on relevant progress.

**Question/topic:** Which internship should the Progress Report cover when I have more than one component?

**Answer:** Cover whichever approved internship component is being completed at the time the Progress Report is written. The course coordinator may specify a different component based on the approved schedule.

## AI use, similarity, and report revision

**Department:** All departments

**Question/topic:** Can AI be used to write an internship report?

**Answer:** The report must be written in the student's own words. AI-generated text is limited to 25% and must be disclosed and cited. Students remain responsible for the accuracy and originality of the entire report.

**Question/topic:** What is the maximum AI percentage?

**Answer:** The maximum is 25%. Exceeding it can result in a Fail and a required rewrite. Students should write independently and keep drafts and evidence of their work.

**Question/topic:** What is the maximum Turnitin similarity percentage?

**Answer:** The maximum similarity threshold is 20%. Cite sources properly and do not copy company descriptions or external text without quotation and reference.

**Question/topic:** Can AI be used only for grammar or wording?

**Answer:** Minor language assistance can still increase the AI-detection result. Review every sentence, disclose AI use when required, and keep the technical content genuinely your own while remaining within the 25% limit.

**Question/topic:** Can a failed Progress Report be revised, and does the first Fail mean failing the course?

**Answer:** A student may be allowed or asked to revise a report. If the revised Progress Report is graded Pass, the earlier Fail does not by itself prevent passing the course. All other course requirements must still be completed.

**Question/topic:** Can students at the same company submit the same report?

**Answer:** No. Each student must write an independent report, even when students work at the same company, team, or project.

## Deadline extensions and petition delays

**Department:** All departments

**Question/topic:** Can I request an extension for a deadline?

**Answer:** Extensions are not automatic. Contact the coordinator before the deadline, explain the reason, provide supporting evidence when relevant, and state when the work can be submitted.

**Question/topic:** Why was my petition rejected or delayed?

**Answer:** Incomplete petitions may be rejected. Check that the request clearly explains the exception and includes the official company or supervisor documents, duration, work description, required approvals, and the required CDC copy.

## Course completion requirements (ECE)

**Department:** Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)

**Question/topic:** How is EECE500 graded and what must be completed to pass?

**Answer:** EECE500 is graded Pass or Fail. Students must complete the approved experience and all applicable Moodle resources, quiz, forms, reports, surveys, employer documentation, and the Final Voice-over Presentation satisfactorily and on time.

## Combined internship arrangements (ECE)

**Department:** Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)

### 6+2 arrangement definition

**Question/topic:** How does a six-week internship plus two-week research arrangement work?

**Answer:** The six company weeks and two faculty-research weeks (two weeks of research with an MSFEA faculty member) must be approved and documented.

### Progress reporting for a 6+2 arrangement

**Question/topic:** When is the Progress Report due for a six-week internship plus two-week research arrangement, and what should it cover?

**Answer:** Submit the Progress Report at the end of Week 4 of the company internship and cover only the company internship in that report.

### Combining two company internships

**Question/topic:** Can two internships be combined to meet the duration requirement?

**Answer:** A combined arrangement requires prior written approval or petition. Each internship must be documented and technically relevant, and the total approved duration must satisfy EECE500 requirements. Use the second-internship forms when instructed.

### 4+4 company-internship split

**Question/topic:** Can EECE500 be split into two four-week internships?

**Answer:** Do not assume a 4+4 split is accepted. It is case-specific and requires a formal petition and written approval before either component is counted.

### Gap between approved components

**Question/topic:** Is a gap allowed between approved internship components?

**Answer:** A gap may be accepted if both components are approved and completed within the permitted period. The complete arrangement must be described in the petition and approved in writing.

## Taking another summer course (ECE)

**Department:** Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)

**Question/topic:** Can I take another summer course while completing EECE500?

**Answer:** Yes, with the required EECE500 and departmental approval. Only when the student takes another summer course, the student must complete ten weeks of approved internship: either ten weeks at one company or six weeks at one company plus four weeks at another company. This ten-week rule does not apply when the student is not taking another summer course. Both companies require the applicable forms and employer documentation.

## Final Report requirements (ECE)

**Department:** Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)

**Question/topic:** How long must the EECE500 Final Training Report be?

**Answer:** The report must be at least five pages and 1,500 words, excluding the cover page, references, and appendix, and must not exceed 20 pages.

**Question/topic:** What formatting should be used for the EECE500 Final Report?

**Answer:** Use double spacing, size 12 font, proper margins and indentation, and page numbers. Label tables, figures, and pictures. Cite external information in the text and include it in the references.

**Question/topic:** What content should the EECE500 Final Report discuss?

**Answer:** Keep the required Moodle report structure. Within it, discuss technical and administrative activities; engineering or computing projects; specific applications of engineering, science, and mathematics; informed judgment about relevant global, economic, environmental, and societal impacts; and how new knowledge was acquired and applied. These are content expectations, not a replacement section structure.

## Reports for multiple approved components (ECE)

**Department:** Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)

**Question/topic:** How should the Final Report cover two internships or an internship plus research?

**Answer:** For two internships, submit one Final Report that clearly separates and discusses each approved component. For a six-week internship plus two-week research arrangement, submit a separate research report for the research component.

## Final Voice-over Presentation duration (ECE)

**Department:** Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)

**Question/topic:** What is the maximum duration of the EECE500 Final Voice-over Presentation?

**Answer:** The presentation may contain no more than five slides, with up to three minutes of recorded audio per slide. The maximum narrated duration is therefore 15 minutes, and students do not need to use the full time. This ECE-specific Voice-over Presentation limit applies instead of the general presentation-duration guidance.
