# Summer Training (Approved Experience) Guidelines — June 2026

## Course Description

The Approved Experience course gives you the opportunity to apply what you have learned in class to real engineering and design challenges, gain professional experience, develop workplace skills, and explore possible career paths.

This internship is a graduation requirement and is normally completed during the summer before your final year.

To successfully pass the course, you must complete the internship requirements, submit all deliverables on time, and follow the regulations outlined in this guide.

## Eligibility

To be eligible to register for the internship (Approved Experience) program, you must have completed a **minimum of 90 credits**.

## Course Registration

Registration and payment deadlines follow the schedule announced by the University Registrar for the summer term:

- Register for XXXX 500 – Approved Experience (the course code prefix depends on your department).
- Pay the required tuition and fees.

## Internship Requirements

To qualify for the Bachelor of Engineering degree, you must complete:

- A minimum of 8 full weeks of approved training.
- Typically equivalent to 320 work hours (8 weeks × 40 hours/week).
- Training at a recognized company, organization, laboratory, or approved research setting in Lebanon or abroad.

### Internships That Are Not Accepted

- **Remote internships are not accepted.** Some departments allow narrow exceptions — see Department-Specific Rules.
- **Internships inside AUB are not accepted**, except in special circumstances.

Any exception to the internship requirements must be approved through a formal petition. Petitions must:

- Be approved by both the Department and the Course Instructor.
- Include all required supporting documents (incomplete petitions will automatically be rejected).
- Be copied (CC) to the MSFEA Career Development Center (CDC) at fcareer@aub.edu.lb.

Submit petitions through the AUB online petition system: https://petitions.aub.edu.lb

Your internship should provide meaningful professional experience related to your field of study and allow you to apply engineering or design knowledge in practice. Requirements may vary slightly by department.

## Securing an Internship through the CDC

The MSFEA CDC office maintains strong relationships with employers and regularly updates job and internship postings. The CDC also supports students applying for competitive international opportunities. Students are strongly encouraged to:

- Stay in contact with the CDC.
- Regularly check internship postings.
- Attend the CDC information session at the beginning of the academic year.

You can find all CDC information relevant to summer internships (list of offers, letter requests, self-secured internship form) on the MSFEA CDC students page: https://www.aub.edu.lb/msfea/cdc/Pages/students.aspx

### Requesting a Letter or Convention de Stage

If the hiring company requires any type of letter or a convention de stage, request one through the CDC letter request form: https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=Glu6x7ZB6UOhIG_2VK2hNxgNyBu3jyVItFyTXDeYtMVUODdKUjhTOTNPMEhJVFJTOExOUjZJWUZNUi4u

The CDC will get back to you as soon as possible.

## Securing an Internship Independently

Students are responsible for securing their own internship opportunities and are encouraged to seek the support of the MSFEA Career Development Center (CDC).

If you secure an internship on your own (outside the CDC), you must complete the CDC's self-secured internship form: https://forms.office.com/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=Glu6x7ZB6UOhIG_2VK2hNxgNyBu3jyVItFyTXDeYtMVUM05XSkRJQThUQ1VaM1dLOEZUOVM4N1Q1RC4u

Training offers not processed through the CDC and not pre-approved by the department will not be counted toward your graduation requirement.

### If the Company Is Already Listed with the CDC

- Submit an online petition requesting approval.
- Upload an official company letter that includes:
  - company letterhead
  - signature
  - internship duration
  - description of the training and expected work

### If the Company Is NOT Listed with the CDC

- Submit an online petition requesting approval.
- Upload an official company letter that explains:
  - the company's work and industry
  - the type of training provided
  - internship duration

## Internships at Startups

Internships at startups may be approved through a formal petition. The petition must clearly explain:

- the scope of work
- the student's responsibilities
- the technical or professional learning outcomes

Approval is required from the Department and the Course Instructor. The CDC Office (fcareer@aub.edu.lb) must also be copied (CC).

## Professional Expectations During the Internship

During your internship, you are expected to behave professionally. This includes:

- punctuality and regular attendance
- professional communication
- respect toward supervisors and coworkers
- responsibility and accountability
- proper workplace conduct

Remember: you are representing yourself, your department, MSFEA, and AUB.

**Keep a daily record of:**

- tasks completed
- projects worked on
- skills learned
- meetings or observations

These notes will help when preparing reports and presentations.

**If problems arise:** If you experience problems during your internship, contact the internship coordinator, your department, and the CDC (if applicable) as soon as possible.

Important: If your assigned work does not match the internship objectives or lacks meaningful engineering/design content, report the issue immediately.

## Important Checklists

### Progress Report Checklist

The Progress Report should follow the template provided on Moodle and is usually around 5 pages.

Your report should include:

- Name and ID Number
- Company Name
- Internship Start and End Dates
- Progress Report Submission Date

Report content:

- **Company Profile** — brief background about the company, its work, and your direct supervisor's contact information.
- **Your Role at the Company** — description of your position, responsibilities, and assigned work.
- **Progress and Contributions** — summary of work completed so far, activities performed, and contributions made.
- **Remaining Tasks** — description of the work and goals remaining for the rest of the internship.

### Final Presentation Guidelines

Depending on department requirements, the presentation may be delivered in person and on campus, or submitted as a recorded presentation.

Presentation requirements:

- Covers your overall internship experience.
- 3–5 minutes presentation time.
- Approximately 3 minutes for questions/discussion.
- Maximum of 5 slides (if PowerPoint is required).
- Voice-over PowerPoint (VOP) may be required by some departments.
- Maximum of 3 minutes audio per slide.

Presentations are typically scheduled during the first two weeks of the Fall semester following the internship.

### Final Training Report

- Submit within 1 week after completing the internship.
- Follow the report template and formatting guidelines provided on Moodle.

### Notice of Completion / Employer Letter Checklist

The Notice of Completion / Employer Letter must:

- Be issued officially by the company (or AUB department if applicable).
- Include internship start and end dates.
- Describe projects and tasks completed.
- Include a brief evaluation of your work.
- Be signed and stamped by the employer/supervisor.

Important: Many companies need time to prepare this document. Request it at least 2–3 weeks before your internship ends.

## Evaluation and Grading

The Approved Experience course is graded as Pass (P) or Fail (F).

Evaluation is based on:

- completion of all required documents
- quality and relevance of the internship experience
- quality of reports
- quality of presentation
- employer evaluation
- professionalism during the internship

Students may be asked to revise reports that do not meet department expectations. Rubrics for reports, presentations, and evaluations are available on Moodle.

## Department-Specific Rules

> These rules differ by department. When a question is affected by department, the answer depends on the student's department.

### Mechanical Engineering (MECH)

- Students may take a summer course while interning if required work hours are completed.
- Internships cannot be split into two separate 4-week periods.
- Technical consulting internships are allowed if related to engineering.
- Finance, banking, or business consulting internships are not accepted.
- Research internships must include hands-on engineering work.
- Programming-focused internships are acceptable if tied to engineering applications.

### Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)

- Students completing a 6-week internship must either complete 2 weeks of research with an MSFEA faculty member or a minimum 4-week internship at another company.
- Students completing a 6+2 arrangement must submit a separate research report for the research component.
- Final training report and final presentation are due within one week after the completion of the internship, based on each student's internship end date.

### Chemical Engineering (CHEM)

- Internship splitting is only allowed within the same summer term.
- Different reporting requirements apply depending on whether internships are with one or multiple companies.
- A recorded 3-minute presentation is required.
- Research internships may be accepted with Chair approval.
- Technical consulting internships are allowed if engineering-related.
- Finance and banking internships are not accepted.

### Industrial Engineering and Management (IEM)

- A 6-week internship may be approved through petition for selected companies.
- Students completing a 6-week internship must either complete 2 weeks of research with an MSFEA faculty member or a minimum 4-week internship at another company.
- Final presentations are generally not required unless specified.
- Selected students may present in seminars or orientation events.
- Remote internships with U.S.-based companies are allowed if the student is legally eligible to work in the U.S. and the internship receives department approval.

### Civil and Environmental Engineering (CEE)

- Internships may be split into two 4-week periods if at least one period is in civil or construction engineering.
- Technical consulting internships are allowed for a maximum of 4 weeks.
- Research internships may be approved with Chair approval.
- Students may take summer courses while completing internship hour requirements.
- A reduced 6-week internship may be approved in exceptional cases for selected companies.
