MSFEA CDC Chatbot — demo page

This page simulates an AUB web page with the chat widget embedded. Click the 💬 bubble in the bottom-right corner and ask an internship / CO-OP question, e.g. "What is the minimum internship duration?" or "What GPA do I need for co-op?".

The widget is a thin client: it just calls the backend POST /chat and renders the answer, its sources, and the disclaimer.
