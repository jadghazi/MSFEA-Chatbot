Approved Experience (Internship)

The Approved Experience is a graduation requirement for MSFEA undergraduate students. This page summarizes the essentials; the assistant at the bottom-right answers detailed questions from the official documents.

Overview

Students complete supervised professional training with an approved employer and submit a set of deliverables through Moodle. Requirements vary slightly by department, so check your department's specific rules before you start.

Key deliverables

WhenDeliverableWhere
First week of trainingNotice of Arrival FormMoodle
During the internshipProfessional Skills RequirementMoodle

First 2 weeks of FallFinal PresentationDepartment

Other CDC programs

  • CO-OP — a longer paid placement with its own eligibility rules.
  • IAESTE — international exchange internships.
  • Full-time job support — CV reviews, interview preparation.
  • Mentorship — guidance from alumni and industry professionals.

Before you email us

Most questions are already answered in the guidelines. Try the assistant first — it answers from those documents and cites the section it used, so you can verify it. If it can't help, it will point you to the right person.
